Gitxsan

The Gitxsan Laxyip reach from Terrace to Hazelton to Smithers — some 35,000 km² of forests, mountains and Anaat (riverways) in Northwest BC.

Tahltan

95,933 km² — the equivalent of 11% of British Columbia. The north and west boundary follows the Alaska–Canada border and extends into the Yukon; the south and east takes in the upper Nass tributaries and the western half of the Stikine plateau, including the sacred headwaters of the Stikine, Nass and Skeena rivers. The communities are Telegraph Creek, Dease Lake and Iskut.

Territory descriptions are drawn from the Nations' own published material — gitxsan.ca and tahltan.org.

From the Founder

Chaz Ware, AScT

Applied Science Technologist  ·  Qualified Environmental Professional

I am Gitxsan and Tahltan, and I have spent twenty years doing environmental work in the territories I come from — alongside good firms, good crews and the Nations who call these territories home.

Ravken exists so that more of this work is done by people from here: building capacity in the communities, teaming with the firms already on the ground, and growing through the partners we work with.

Projects go better when Nations, industry and the crews in the field are working toward the same result. A strong economy, strong communities and resilient ecosystems are not competing goals. Ravken is where my culture and my career finally point the same direction.

Chaz Ware, AScT  ·  Founder & CEO Gitxsan  ·  Tahltan

How We Work

What we stand on

Safety and integrity are not compliance line items for us — they are why clients, partners and Nations can hand us high-stakes work and trust it will come back done right.

Working Together

More benefit, and more range of skill and view, comes from working with others over the long term than from going it alone. We team, we subcontract, and we stay.

Building Capacity in the Nations

Every project should leave more qualified people in the communities than it found. Hiring, training and mentoring members is part of the scope, not an add-on.

Tied to the Land

We work where we come from. What happens to the water and the ground happens to us, and we treat it that way.

Doing What Is Right

What we tell a client, a Nation or a regulator is what they can rely on. When the right call costs us, we make it anyway.

Zero-Incident Standard

Safety is non-negotiable — the only acceptable outcome.

Common Sense, Constructable Solutions

The highest probability of success at the lowest cost and least complexity.
