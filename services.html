Services

What major projects need, from the first assessment to the last reclamation report

Mines, pipelines, transmission lines and highways across Northwest BC are permitted, built, operated and closed in stages. Ravken provides the environmental field work each stage needs — baseline studies and permitting support, monitoring against permit conditions, water quality and effluent compliance, and reclamation — delivered by qualified staff under a Qualified Environmental Professional, inside the client's management systems.

Assessment & Permitting

Baseline StudiesAquatic and terrestrial baselines — water quality, fish and fish habitat, wildlife, vegetation and soils — collected early enough to support the environmental assessment and permit applications instead of delaying them.
Exploration & Early Works MonitoringEnvironmental monitoring of drill programs, trails, camps, access roads and early works under notice-of-work and pre-construction conditions, so the ground is left in the state the permit requires.
Permitting SupportField data, mitigation plans and compliance planning for Mines Act, Environmental Management Act, Water Sustainability Act and Fisheries Act authorizations and environmental assessment conditions — prepared to survive review by the regulator and by the Nation whose territory the work is on.
Nation-Side Review and OversightTechnical review of proponent applications and permit submissions for a Nation government, referrals support, and Nation-side monitoring of a proponent's performance against permit and certificate conditions.

Construction & Operations

Construction MonitoringEnvironmental monitoring during construction — right-of-way clearing and grading, watercourse crossings, plant sites, tailings and water management facilities, diversions and haul roads — with problems raised on site before they become a stop-work order.
Water Quality & Effluent ComplianceTurbidity and instream monitoring at works near water; surface water, groundwater and effluent sampling against permit limits, including the Metal and Diamond Mining Effluent Regulations; reporting that a regulator and a monitoring committee can rely on.
Wildlife ProgramsFish and amphibian salvages, nesting bird sweeps, and bat, bird and raptor surveys — timed to clearing windows so crews keep moving.
Sediment & ErosionDesign, installation and supply of erosion and sediment control measures on pads, roads and stockpiles.

Closure & Reclamation

Progressive ReclamationReclamation of exhausted pads, roads and disturbed ground while the mine is still operating, and closure reclamation at the end — to a condition the regulator and the Nation are both prepared to accept.
Closure MonitoringPost-closure water quality, revegetation and erosion monitoring, and the reporting that shows closure conditions are being met.
Effectiveness MonitoringLonger-term tracking of reclamation and habitat restoration performance to verify that what was built is holding.

Corridors & Linear Projects

Pipelines, Transmission & HighwaysConstruction environmental monitoring on active spreads and corridors — clearing windows, crossings, reinstatement — inside the prime contractor's environmental management system and reporting cadence.
Access & Resource RoadsEnvironmental inspection of road construction, maintenance and deactivation, including the access that serves a mine or camp.

Every scope is delivered by qualified staff under a Qualified Environmental Professional, inside the client's environmental management and safety systems. Ravken is the monitoring and compliance layer: we do not perform construction, excavation, remediation or hazardous waste handling.
