Environmental services for the projects breaking ground across Northwest British Columbia.

Ravken is owned by a member of the Gitxsan and Tahltan Nations and works alongside Nations, their development corporations, prime contractors and established environmental firms. We add qualified, local capacity to the teams already doing this work — and the best results come from everyone at the table working together over the long term.
